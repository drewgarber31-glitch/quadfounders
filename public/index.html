Nobody has done it yet? Good. Here's how you start.

A brand-new idea has no template to copy. So instead of guessing, your plan starts with what has to be true and the cheapest way to find out.

01

Name the assumptions

The few things that must be true for your idea to work.

Example: Students fly out on the same days.
02

Test the riskiest first

Talk to people who have the problem before you spend money.

Example: Ask 10 people how they got to the airport last break.
03

Mark what you learned

Confirmed or proven wrong. Both are progress.

Example: Confirmed: 7 of 10 left the same week.
04

Adjust the plan

Ask the co-pilot what to change when an assumption breaks.

Example: Focus on the Friday before break.

This customer discovery approach comes from guidance published by an NSF I-Corps Hub at the University of Alaska Fairbanks. Quad Founders can't promise any idea will work. It helps you find out quickly and cheaply.

Straight answers

What Quad Founders is, and isn't.

It is

  • A step-by-step plan built around your idea
  • Educational guidance that links to official sources
  • A website you download and own
  • AI drafts that are clearly labeled and fully editable

It isn't

  • A lawyer, accountant, or financial advisor
  • A promise that your business will make money
  • A place to enter ID, bank, or card numbers
  • A host for your website. It lives on your own account.
FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is this legal or tax advice?

No. Quad Founders gives educational guidance only. Legal and tax steps link to the official government source so you can read the rules yourself. For your specific situation, check with a qualified professional. Free mentors are available through SCORE.

Does it work outside North Carolina?

Yes. Pick your state or territory when you start: all 50 states, DC, Puerto Rico, the U.S. Virgin Islands, Guam, American Samoa, and the Northern Mariana Islands. Your legal steps then link to that state's official registration, tax, licensing, and free business help offices, found through the IRS and SBA state lists. North Carolina has the most detail so far. For other states, Quad Founders has not checked state-specific rules, so the co-pilot points you to those offices instead of guessing. Federal guidance, like the IRS, FTC, and USPTO pages, applies everywhere.

What if nobody has done my idea before?

That's what it's built for. Your plan is shaped around your idea instead of a template, and it starts with the assumptions that have to be true and a cheap way to test each one.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. You describe your idea, answer a few questions, and ask for changes in plain English. The website is written for you.

Who owns my website?

You do. You download the files and publish them on your own account. Quad Founders doesn't host your site.

Is my idea private?

Your plan is saved so you can come back to it. Anyone with your plan's link can open it, so only share the link with people you trust. Your idea and answers are sent to an AI provider to write your plan. Never enter personal details like ID, bank, or card numbers.
