Privacy

What we store, and how to delete it

Quad Founders has no accounts and never handles payments. Here is everything it keeps, in plain language.

If you build a plan

  • What's stored: your idea, your answers, your plan and its progress, your chats with the co-pilot, your test site's text, and any contact info you type into it (email, Instagram, location).
  • Who can see it: anyone who has your plan's link. There's no login, so treat that link like a password and don't share it. Your test site has a separate live link that shows only the site, never the plan.
  • AI: to write your plan, walkthroughs, chat replies, and site, the text you enter is sent to the AI provider Quad Founders uses (Anthropic).
  • On your device: your browser remembers which plans you opened and the state you picked, so you can find them again. This stays in your browser.
  • Never asked for or stored: Social Security numbers, ID numbers, bank or card numbers, or passwords.

If you sign up on a student's test site

  • What's stored: the name and email you enter, your answer to the site's optional question, and the date.
  • Who can see it: the student who made that site, through their private plan link. Quad Founders doesn't email you, sell your information, or share it with anyone else.
  • Who is responsible for contacting you: the student. Their site is a student-run business idea, not Quad Founders.
  • To be removed: email the address shown on that site's sign-up form. The student can delete your sign-up at any time.
  • Age: you must be 13 or older to sign up.

Limits and spam protection

To stop abuse and control costs, Quad Founders keeps daily counters tied to a one-way scrambled (hashed) version of your network address. The address itself isn't stored.

Deleting things

  • A whole plan: open the plan and use Delete plan at the bottom of the overview. It permanently deletes the plan, its chats, its test site, and all of its sign-ups.
  • One sign-up or all of them: open your test site in the app and use Delete or Delete all under Sign-ups.

Where it runs

Quad Founders runs on Cloudflare. These Quad Founders pages load fonts from Google Fonts. Student test sites load no outside scripts or fonts.
